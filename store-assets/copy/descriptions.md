# FitFlow Store Description

FitFlow is your all-in-one fitness companion.

## AI Workouts

Personalised workout plans based on your goal, fitness level and progress.

## Nutrition Tracking

Log meals quickly and follow your daily calories and macros.

## Community

Share workouts, follow friends and stay motivated with fitness streaks.

## Progress

Track your weekly and monthly fitness progress with clear analytics.

FitFlow brings workouts, nutrition, community and progress tracking together in one simple experience.
